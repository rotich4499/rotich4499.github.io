1. Effects of Temperature and Intrinsic Structural Defects on Mechanical Properties of Niobium Nitrate

This undergraduate research project focused on studying how temperature and defects affect NbN, a material used in grinding machines. The goal was to understand mechanical behavior under thermal stress conditions.
