RASESMA 2023 - Nairobi

RASESMA 2023 will be hosted by the Technical and Applied Physics Department at the School of Physics and Earth Science, located in lab N211, on the second floor of the N-block at the university. To reach the venue, enter through the public entrance on Workshop Road. Access to the university requires registration with security at the gate, and your destination is N301, 3rd Floor, N-Block in the Physics Department. There are no access restrictions to enter the laboratory.

Setting up Yambo, QE, and Tutorials

To follow the school sessions, you need a running version of the Yambo/QE codes along with the necessary tutorial files and databases. Detailed instructions on installing the codes can be found in the dedicated installation guide page. Once installed, follow the tutorial setup instructions to start working on the exercises.

Event Schedule

Sunday 19 Feb: For those already in Nairobi, a get-together and Yambo installation session will be held at the YMCA South C starting from 18:00. If you're interested, please send an email to rasesma2023@tukenya.ac.ke to confirm your participation.

More Details

The event will cover key topics in Quantum Mechanics, Math, Theoretical Approaches, and Approximations, along with exercises and exchange programs.
