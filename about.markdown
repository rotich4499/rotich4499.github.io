👤 About Me

Charles Kipchumba Rotich is a Master's student in Physics at the Technical University of Kenya, specializing in Material Science. He is a member of the Material Modelling Group led by Prof. George Amollo. Charles holds a Bachelor of Technology in Physics from the Technical University of Kenya and is currently participating in the Advanced Physics Training in Collaboration with Africa (APhRICA) program at ICTP-EAIFR, Kigali.

Charles has a strong background in computational material science, particularly in the study of 2D magnetic semiconductors. His work involves first-principles calculations using Quantum ESPRESSO and SIESTA codes, with a focus on properties such as spin-orbit coupling, magnetic anisotropy, and exchange interactions. He has also carried out simulations to compute mechanical properties, including bulk modulus and elastic constants, as well as electronic properties like band structures.

He recently completed the ALX AI Career Essentials course, acquiring foundational knowledge in machine learning and AI, and is passionate about integrating these tools with density functional theory to accelerate materials discovery.
