👤 About Me

I'm Charles Rotich, a dedicated physicist and researcher with a passion for material science, currently pursuing a Master's in Physics at the Technical University of Kenya. My academic work focuses on 2D magnetic semiconductors, using computational tools like Quantum ESPRESSO and SIESTA to investigate spin-orbit coupling, magnetic anisotropy, and magnetic moments.

I'm currently enrolled in the Advanced Physics Training in Collaboration with Africa (APhRICA) program, a one-year initiative hosted at ICTP-EAIFR, Kigali, aimed at advancing theoretical and computational expertise in cutting-edge physics topics.

During my undergraduate studies, I conducted a project titled "Effects of Temperature and Intrinsic Structural Defects on Mechanical Properties of Niobium Nitrate," which analyzed how thermal and structural variations impact the durability of niobium nitrate in high-stress applications like grinding machine components.

Additionally, I completed the ALX AI Career Essentials course, where I gained foundational knowledge in machine learning and artificial intelligence, including the ethical use of AI, core ML concepts, and the future of intelligent systems.

I’m passionate about leveraging simulation, data, and theory to explore the potential of advanced materials in solving real-world challenges.
