Charles Kipchumba Rotich

Computational physicist · Materials science researcher

I use first-principles calculations to study the electronic, optical and surface properties of materials, with a focus on oxides for photocatalytic water splitting.

I am an MSc Physics student and Graduate Research Assistant at The Technical University of Kenya, currently undertaking Erasmus+ research mobility at the University of Cantabria in Santander, Spain.

Research interests

  • First-principles modelling and density functional theory.
  • Electronic structure of silicon, carbon diamond and benzene: band structures, electronic gaps and fractional charge, alongside density of states (DOS) and projected density of states (PDOS).
  • Origins of band gaps in perovskite oxides, including the roles of structural and magnetic symmetry.
  • Electronic and optical properties of oxides, surface science and photocatalytic water splitting.
  • Mechanical properties of niobium nitride and the effects of temperature and vacancy defects.
