# My Projects

## 1. 2D Magnetic Semiconductors
Currently researching the properties of 2D magnetic semiconductors using DFT simulations. Materials under study include CrTe, MnS₂, and CrI₃, utilizing Quantum ESPRESSO and SIESTA for electronic structure and magnetic property calculations. 

## 2. Effects of Temperature and Intrinsic Structural Defects on Mechanical Properties of Niobium Nitrate
This project focused on analyzing how thermal stress and material defects affect Niobium Nitrate (NbN), commonly used in grinding equipment.
